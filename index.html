Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science & Applied Mathematics @ Iowa State University

Hi! I am Mojdeh. I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics at Iowa State University, advised by Prof. Baskar Ganapathysubramanian and Prof. James Rossmanith. My research lies at the intersection of scientific machine learning, probabilistic modeling, and computational science.

I develop machine-learning methods that make scientific models faster, more uncertainty-aware, and more useful for large-scale discovery and decision making. My work includes probabilistic emulation of mechanistic simulations, uncertainty quantification, out-of-distribution detection, reinforcement learning, differentiable simulation, and causal inference.

My current research uses AI to accelerate mechanistic crop simulation and explore large spaces of environmental and biological configurations. More broadly, I am interested in methods that combine data-driven learning with scientific structure to enable reliable discovery in complex dynamical systems.

Previously, I worked on causal inference with missing data, fairness in ensemble learning, deep-learning-based insect recognition, functional data analysis, and public-health data science.

Research

Developing surrogate, emulator, and differentiable simulation models that transform computationally expensive mechanistic systems into scalable tools for scientific exploration, optimization, and what-if analysis.
  • Uncertainty, Robustness & Distribution Shift. Quantifying predictive uncertainty and detecting out-of-distribution inputs so machine-learning systems can communicate when their predictions may be unreliable.
  • Learning and Decision Making in Dynamical Systems.Developing machine-learning approaches for systems that evolve over time, with a focus on reinforcement learning for sequential decision making under uncertainty.
  • Causal & Statistical Learning. Studying causal-effect identification under missing data and statistical methods for complex, incomplete observational systems.
